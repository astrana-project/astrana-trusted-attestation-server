# Relationship types

Every relationship an organisation grants has one of these types. The values are fixed English identifiers, the same
whichever organisation uses them and never translated. `shared/contract/relationship-types.json` holds the list, with a
display label for each locale. Every implementation loads it.
[Decision record 14](adr/0014-relationship-types-are-a-fixed-vocabulary-in-one-shared-file.md) says why the vocabulary
is fixed, and [decision record 15](adr/0015-relationship-types-are-admitted-by-fixed-tests-with-no-catch-all-value.md)
gives the six tests a new value must pass.

There is no `other` value. If a relationship does not fit any type exactly, grant it under the closest one. A level or
designation within a relationship, such as "Fellow" within a professional body, goes in the subtype, which the
organisation sets as free text.

| Value                          | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `advisor`                      | A formally appointed advisory role, for example on a government advisory council or a not-for-profit advisory board. It gives advice only, with no governance or decision-making authority. Distinct from `director`, which has governance authority, and from `official`, which implies government office or authority rather than a consultative role. |
| `apprentice`                   | A formal trade or vocational training relationship, typically contract-based or regulated, distinct from both `student` and `employee`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `business_owner`               | Registered ownership or control of a company, attested by a companies registration body or a government department, not by the company itself.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `citizen`                      | Citizenship of a country, attested by the state that grants it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `client`                       | A customer of the organisation, such as a bank's account holder or a telecommunications company subscriber.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `contractor`                   | Engaged by the organisation to provide work or services under a contract, not employment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `director`                     | A formal governance or oversight role, such as a company or not-for-profit board director, distinct from employment and usually unpaid and non-executive.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `donor`                        | A recognised or disclosed benefactor relationship, for example published donor recognition or a legally registered political donation, attested by the recipient organisation or a donation-disclosure authority. Private or anonymous giving has no authoritative basis for attestation.                                                                |
| `employee`                     | Employed by the organisation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `graduate`                     | Holds a qualification the organisation awarded.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `intellectual_property_holder` | Registered patent or trademark ownership, attested by a patent or trademark office. Distinct from `property_owner`, a different registry and a different kind of asset.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `licensed_professional`        | Holds a professional licence or registration from the body that issues it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `member`                       | A member of the organisation, such as a club, society, union, professional body or congregation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `official`                     | A government-issued relationship that is not employment, for example an elected or appointed role.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `organiser`                    | An informal organisational or committee role in a club, society or volunteer group, for example a committee member or an event organiser. Distinct from `director`, since it carries no fiduciary or legal governance duty, and from ordinary `member`, since it carries organisational responsibility.                                                  |
| `partner`                      | A legal co-owner, for example a partner in a law or accounting firm, not employment. Distinct from `employee` in kind, not only in seniority.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `property_owner`               | Registered ownership of real property, attested by a land titles or property registry, on the same registry-verified basis as `business_owner`, `shareholder` and `intellectual_property_holder`.                                                                                                                                                        |
| `religious_leader`             | A recognised spiritual or religious authority, such as clergy, a monastic or equivalent, distinct from general congregation or community membership.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `resident`                     | Authoritatively attested to live at or in a place, at any scale. That can be civic, such as government residency or immigration status, or a specific property, attested by a landlord, body corporate or property manager. It does not imply a formal leasehold. Distinct from `tenant`.                                                                |
| `shareholder`                  | A registered equity stake that does not necessarily imply control, attested by a share registry, a transfer agent or a companies registration body. Distinct from `business_owner`.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `student`                      | Enrolled with the organisation to study.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `tenant`                       | A formal leasehold relationship, typically backed by a registered lease and, in many jurisdictions, a bond lodged with a tenancy authority. Distinct from `resident`, since it carries specific legal standing rather than occupancy alone.                                                                                                              |
| `trustee`                      | A formal trust or fiduciary role, distinct from `director`. May be attested by a trust or companies registration body rather than by the organisation itself.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `veteran`                      | Military service, typically past rather than current.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `volunteer`                    | Gives time to the organisation's work without pay.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
